Privacy policy

What this site does with your information, in plain English.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy covers the website https://mountinr-gif.github.io/cupofcoffee-korean/, published by Cup of Coffee Korean. It is written to be read — if anything is unclear, ask us on Instagram and we will explain it.

1. Who is responsible

Cup of Coffee Korean operates this site and is the controller of any personal information described here. The contact channel for privacy questions is the Instagram account @cupofcoffee_korean. We do not publish a postal address; if a legal notice requires one, it will be provided on request through that channel.

2. What this site collects today

Only what you type into the newsletter form. There are no accounts, no login, no comment system, no analytics and no advertising on this site, and we do not set cookies ourselves. Everything that runs in your browser — the pronunciation player, the romanization toggle, the quizzes — runs locally on your device.

The phrase card PDF is a direct download: nothing is asked for, and nothing is recorded, when you take it.

Two things are stored in your browser, both on your device and never sent to us: whether you prefer romanization hidden or shown, and nothing else. They disappear when you clear your browser data.

3. Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and may later also be served through Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, these providers process the technical details of your request — IP address, browser type, the page requested, the time — in their server logs for security and reliability. We do not receive or keep those logs. Their handling is governed by their own privacy policies (GitHub: GitHub Privacy Statement; Cloudflare: Cloudflare Privacy Policy).

4. Fonts and audio

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests files from Google’s servers and Google may see your IP address. Korean pronunciation is generated by your own browser’s speech engine; the text you ask it to read is not sent to us.

5. The newsletter

The newsletter form on this site is live and is operated through Kit (formerly ConvertKit), a mailing service based in the United States that stores the address for us. When you submit the form:

  • Your email address is sent to Kit, together with a first name if you choose to type one. Nothing else is collected — no phone number, no address, no tracking profile.
  • Nothing is sent until you press the button yourself.
  • Kit may send you a confirmation email first (double opt-in); the address is only added to the list once it is confirmed.
  • Every email includes a one-click unsubscribe link, and you can leave at any time without giving a reason.
  • The form submits to Kit’s own endpoint (https://app.kit.com/forms/9209507/subscriptions), so your browser talks to Kit directly.

Your address will not be sold, rented or shared with anyone else for their own marketing. Kit’s handling of it is described in the Kit privacy policy. To have your address deleted, unsubscribe from any email or message us on Instagram and we will remove it.

The paid daily letter is a separate subscription sold on Gumroad. Buying it means giving Gumroad your email and payment details under Gumroad’s own privacy policy and terms; we never see or store your card number.

6. Advertising

This site currently shows no advertising and loads no advertising scripts. If advertising is added later — for example Google AdSense or Journey by Mediavine — this policy will be updated before any ad script is loaded, and the following will apply:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info.
  • Where the law requires consent (for example in the EEA or the UK), a consent banner will appear before any non-essential cookie is set.

7. Affiliate links

There are no affiliate links on this site today. If travel, book or learning partners are added, the links will be labelled as affiliate links at the point where they appear, and the price you pay will not change.

8. Links to other services

This site links to Instagram, to Kit for the newsletter form, and to Gumroad (where the paid daily letter is sold), and to our own subscription landing page. Once you follow a link, the destination’s own privacy policy applies. We do not control what those services collect.

9. Children

The lessons are written for a general audience of language learners and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, contact us and it will be deleted.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to its processing, or to receive a copy. In practice, today we hold nothing beyond a newsletter address if you have signed up. To exercise any of these rights, message us on Instagram and we will respond within 30 days.

11. Changes to this policy

When something changes — most recently when the newsletter and the phrase-card download were switched on in November 2026 — this page will be updated and the date at the top changed. Material changes will also be mentioned in the newsletter.

12. Contact

Privacy questions: @cupofcoffee_korean on Instagram.
