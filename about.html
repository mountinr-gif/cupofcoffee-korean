About

The sentence is the lesson.

Cup of Coffee Korean is a Korean-learning project that started as one sentence a day on Instagram and grew into these longer, slower lessons. Everything here is written for people who want to use Korean, not admire it from a distance.

How it started

It began with a daily card: one Korean sentence, a romanization, a translation, and a picture. The rule was simple — the sentence had to be something a person could actually say that day. Not a grammar example. Not a textbook line. Something you would use at a ticket window, at a table, at a counter.

That rule still runs the site. Each lesson takes one real situation in Korea and builds outwards from it: the phrases first, then what they sound like, then why the place works the way it does, then three questions to check you were paying attention.

The Instagram channel (@cupofcoffee_korean) is still the front door and still posts daily. This site is where a lesson can be long enough to be useful.

Who writes it

A Korean teacher based in Seoul writes and checks every sentence on this site. The characters came from the channel: the teacher you see in the videos, the girl who is always holding a coffee, and the poodle who appears in almost every frame and has never once been given a line.

If a phrase on this site is wrong, it is a mistake and not a style choice — tell us and it gets fixed. That offer is the whole quality-control system of a small site, and it works surprisingly well.
